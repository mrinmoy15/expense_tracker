Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filtering. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third-party advertisers or analytics providers. We may use infrastructure services (such as hosting providers) that process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your data, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
